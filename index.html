Chhath Radio is a simple, free playlist experience for timeless and modern Chhath geet. Open the page, press play, and keep the music running — no signup or app required.

What is Chhath Radio?

A browser-based Chhath music player built for easy, uninterrupted festival listening.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. The page is responsive and the player controls are designed to remain easy to use on smaller screens.

Where does the music play from?

The songs play through YouTube. Chhath Radio does not host or redistribute the music files.
